About Future Smart Support

We build production-grade AI agents for support teams. Our approach is simple: keep your data in your systems, connect via MCP connectors, craft robust prompts & policies, then observe and iterate with analytics.

How it works

1

Connect via MCP

Use MCP clients/servers to securely expose your knowledge bases (e.g., docs, CRM, wiki) and tools (e.g., ticketing, order status) to the agent.

2

Design prompts & policies

Guardrails, escalation rules, tone, language, and workflows are baked into modular prompts with clear fallbacks.

3

Deploy everywhere

Serve the same agent on chat, email, and voice. Log actions, track KPIs, and continuously improve with feedback loops.
